Drop in any song. Fake Maker listens, then forges a fake sheet: tempo, key, chords, progressions, song map, lyrics, scales and the main licks as real tab images, on screen and as a printable PDF. Everything runs in your browser, so your audio never leaves your machine.

How this works & what to expect

Fake Maker uses signal processing in your browser: an onset-based beat tracker for tempo, a pitch-class (chroma) analysis with template matching for chords, a self-similarity map for song structure, and a pitch tracker that finds repeated melodic phrases and fingers them on a standard-tuned guitar. Lyrics use a small Whisper speech model running locally.

It is an assistant, not an oracle. Tempo, chords and sections are very good on clear band recordings and rougher on dense mixes, live tempo drift, or heavy distortion. Licks are most accurate when a single guitar or voice carries the line. Use the tempo, time signature and editable section and lyric fields to correct anything, and the PDF follows your edits.
