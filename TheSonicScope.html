💡 Compositional Development & Opportunities
Secondary Dominant Substitution (V7/iv)
Introducing a B7 or B7(b9) before moving to the minor iv chord (Am) injects strong chromatic tension and classical voice-leading.
Modal Interchange / Borrowed Chords
Try substituting the major G chord with a borrowed Cmaj7 or Fmaj7 from the parallel E major/minor Dorian mode for a lush, cinematic texture shift.
Relative Major Modulation (Bridge Opportunity)
Transitioning into the relative major key (G major) for the chorus or bridge section opens up uplifting melodic arcs before returning to the dark moody home key of E minor.
Pedal Point Bass Line
Maintain an open low E string as a continuous pedal tone underneath the chord sequence (Em – G/E – D/E – Am/E) to create suspension and atmospheric tension.
