🧭 How to use the results

Start with the suggested tonal centre, then compare the candidate chords against your actual riff. Try one new chord at a time, preserve the riff's rhythmic identity, and listen for how the melody's notes interact with the new harmony.

Distorted guitars, bends, drop tunings, bass notes and layered recordings can confuse automated pitch estimates. A low-confidence result is a prompt to experiment, not a reason to discard your ears.
