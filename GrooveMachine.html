How this version works

V1

• Upload a riff for playback alongside the band.

• Choose a style, root note, tempo, feel, and length.

• Change the mixer levels and mute individual instruments.

• Render a separate accompaniment MP3 for offline practice.

• This version generates a repeating accompaniment pattern. It does not yet transcribe your riff, detect chords, or automatically compose a song structure from the uploaded audio.
