How this version works

BETA 2

• Upload a riff for playback alongside the band.

• Choose a style, root note, tempo, feel, and length.

• Change the mixer levels and mute individual instruments.

• Render a separate accompaniment MP3 for offline practice.

• Choose a chord progression to generate changing harmony, bass movement and keyboard parts.

• The original audio can start with the backing band, but automatic tempo alignment and chord detection are not yet implemented.

• Soundfont instruments require internet access. Drum sounds are still synthesized; realistic drum sample kits are a future upgrade.

Backing Track Studio

Build, preview, and export a chord-based backing track.

Progression Library

Progressions are saved in this browser. Loading one keeps your current mix.

Supported chords: C, Cm, C7, Cmaj7, Cm7, Csus4, Cdim, C6, C9, F#m7, Bbmaj7 and similar. The sequence repeats to fill the selected bars. Enter your own progression; it does not need to match the key selector.

02 / Instrument ensemble & playing styles

SAMPLED SOUNDFONTS

Choose each instrument and its note pattern independently. Soundfonts are General MIDI quality; playing styles change MIDI note timing and duration, not true recorded articulations. For genuine pizzicato strings, select Pizzicato strings. Synth drums remain basic. First render requires internet.
